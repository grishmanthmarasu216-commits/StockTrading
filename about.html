Our Heritage

A technology firm operating in finance

Traditional brokerages were built on high commissions, cold calls, pushy relationship managers, and bloated offices. TradeNest took the opposite path: zero sales calls, zero advertising gimmicks, and complete focus on building the cleanest trading technology possible.

Over 75% of our workforce are software engineers, system architects, and UX designers. We treat investing as a utility—it should be instant, reliable, and completely transparent about its underlying costs.
